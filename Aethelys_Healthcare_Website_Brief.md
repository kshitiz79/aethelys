# Aethelys Healthcare --- Website Strategy & Development Brief

**Project:** Corporate website for Aethelys Healthcare\
**Reference websites:** [MedSwift Global](https://medswiftglobal.com/) ·
[AnyMed Services](https://www.anymedindia.com/)\
**Recommended stack:** Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS\
**Document purpose:** Content, sitemap, visual direction, and
implementation requirements for the website consultant/development team.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Brand and positioning

### Recommended positioning

**Aethelys Healthcare is a healthcare services and solutions partner
that helps hospitals and healthcare organizations build dependable
teams, strengthen operations, and access technology and infrastructure
support.**

The website should present Aethelys as a professional B2B service
provider---not as a hospital, clinic, or direct-to-patient medical
provider.

### Suggested tagline options

-   **Stronger Healthcare Operations. Better Care Delivery.**
-   Enabling Healthcare Through People, Processes & Technology.
-   Your Partner in Healthcare Workforce and Infrastructure Solutions.

Use the first tagline as the working recommendation unless the brand
team has an approved line.

### Primary audiences

-   Hospitals and multi-specialty healthcare groups
-   Clinics, diagnostic centres and day-care facilities
-   Healthcare entrepreneurs and hospital promoters
-   Government and institutional healthcare projects
-   Healthcare technology and infrastructure partners

### Main website objective

Generate qualified business enquiries from organizations that need
staffing, outsourced operational support, or data-centre/technology
solutions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Reference website analysis

### MedSwift Global --- breadth of healthcare services

Reference: https://medswiftglobal.com/

Useful patterns to adapt: - A broad healthcare solutions portfolio
presented through clear service categories. - Consulting, hospital
setup, medical equipment, HMS/EHR, biomedical maintenance, medical
staffing and pharmaceutical/procurement capabilities. - A
partnership-led message that communicates end-to-end support. - Service
pages that explain scope and practical deliverables.

**What Aethelys should take from this:** communicate the breadth of its
service capabilities, use clear service cards, and explain how each
offering helps healthcare institutions.

### AnyMed Services --- hospital operations and accountability

Reference: https://www.anymedindia.com/

Useful patterns to adapt: - A strong headline focused on end-to-end
hospital operations. - Clear explanations of operational responsibility
and institutional outcomes. - Workforce, financial management,
compliance, technology and biomedical support presented as connected
capabilities. - Trust-building through expertise, structured processes,
governance and measurable performance.

**What Aethelys should take from this:** present services as business
solutions with defined scope, service-level expectations, reporting and
accountability---not just a list of activities.

### Aethelys differentiation

Combine the breadth of a healthcare solutions partner with the clarity
of an operations-focused service provider. Keep the brand's actual
service lines prominent and avoid claiming capabilities, certifications,
partnerships or results that have not been verified.

## 3. Recommended service architecture

The following structure is a proposed information architecture based on
the service direction supplied for this brief. Confirm exact service
scope with the Aethelys team before publishing.

### A. Healthcare Staffing Solutions

Provide qualified and role-appropriate workforce support to healthcare
institutions.

Potential service areas: - Doctors and specialist hiring support, where
authorized and applicable - Nursing staff and nursing support teams -
Paramedical and allied healthcare professionals - Technicians, including
laboratory and radiology roles - Hospital administration and
front-office personnel - Medical records, billing and healthcare
operations staff - Housekeeping, facility and non-clinical support
staff - Temporary, contract, project-based and permanent staffing models

Possible delivery process: 1. Understand the institution's staffing
requirement. 2. Define role profiles, qualifications, experience and
shift requirements. 3. Source and screen candidates. 4. Verify
credentials and references as appropriate to the role and applicable
law. 5. Coordinate interviews and selection. 6. Support onboarding,
deployment and replacement processes. 7. Review attendance, service
quality and client feedback.

**Important:** Do not imply that Aethelys independently verifies
clinical competence or provides clinical services unless the
organization has the required processes, personnel and legal authority.

### B. Outsourced Healthcare Services

Help hospitals outsource selected support functions so internal teams
can focus on core clinical and institutional priorities.

Potential service areas, subject to actual capability: - Front-office
and patient helpdesk operations - Patient registration and appointment
coordination - Billing and administrative support - Medical records and
documentation support - Housekeeping and facility support - Hospital
attendants and non-clinical support personnel - Operations coordination
and manpower management - Helpdesk, back-office and shared-service
support

Each service page should specify: - What is included and excluded -
Typical deployment model - Client responsibilities and Aethelys
responsibilities - Supervision and escalation structure - Reporting and
review cadence - Relevant service-level indicators

### C. Data Centre & Healthcare IT Infrastructure Solutions

Position this as a technology and infrastructure service line for
healthcare organizations. Only publish the specific solutions Aethelys
is equipped to deliver.

Potential service areas: - Data-centre planning and infrastructure
consulting - Server, storage and network infrastructure support -
Infrastructure deployment and migration coordination - Backup and
disaster-recovery planning - Infrastructure monitoring and managed
support - Availability, capacity and performance management - Healthcare
application hosting coordination - Secure connectivity between
healthcare facilities - Support for hospital information systems
(HIS/HMS), EHR and related platforms through qualified technology
partners

Healthcare-specific messaging: - Support reliable access to critical
information systems. - Plan for continuity, backup and recovery. -
Design infrastructure around operational needs and data sensitivity. -
Coordinate with the client's IT, security and compliance teams.

**Security and compliance note:** Avoid absolute claims such as "100%
secure" or "fully compliant" unless supported by documented controls,
audits and applicable certifications. Do not claim that Aethelys is a
certified data-centre operator unless this is verified.

### D. Healthcare Operations & Consulting (optional / confirm scope)

If Aethelys offers these capabilities, add: - Hospital operations
assessment - Workflow and process improvement - SOP development and
implementation support - Manpower planning - Vendor and facility
coordination - Project implementation support - Operational reporting
and performance reviews

If these are not current services, do not show them as active offerings.
They can be added later as the business expands.

planned around continuity and data sensitivity. - **Long-term
partnership:** periodic reviews and service improvement discussions.

These are positioning statements; publish them only to the extent they
reflect actual company practices.

3.  **Plan** --- Agree on deployment, responsibilities, service levels
    and escalation.
4.  **Deliver** --- Coordinate the agreed staffing or service solution.
5.  **Review** --- Monitor agreed indicators and identify improvements.

## 6. Visual design direction

### Overall feel

Professional, modern, calm and enterprise-ready. The design should
combine healthcare credibility with the clarity of a technology and
operations partner.

### Suggested palette

  Usage        Colour
  ------------ ---------------------------
  Primary      Deep navy `#12304A`
  Secondary    Healthcare teal `#138A8A`
  Accent       Soft aqua `#DDF3F1`
  Background   Off-white `#F7FAFC`
  Body text    Slate `#405264`
  Highlight    Muted blue `#E8F0F7`

These are starting points; confirm against the Aethelys logo and brand
guidelines.

### Typography

-   Headings: Manrope or Plus Jakarta Sans
-   Body: Inter
-   Use a clear hierarchy, generous line spacing and readable body
    sizes.

### UI principles

-   Spacious layouts and strong visual hierarchy
-   Rounded cards with subtle borders, not excessive shadows
-   Simple line icons for service categories
-   Real, licensed healthcare and infrastructure photography
-   Consistent CTA styling
-   Subtle transitions; avoid distracting animations
-   Accessible contrast and visible keyboard focus states
-   Responsive layouts designed mobile-first

### Image direction

Use authentic, professional imagery showing: - Healthcare professionals
in a hospital environment - Workforce coordination and administrative
teams - IT infrastructure, server racks and network operations -
Hospital operations meetings and technology collaboration

Use licensed images or original photography. Do not use another
company's logo, team photos or proprietary graphics.

### Component expectations

Build reusable components for: - Header and responsive navigation - Hero
section - Service cards - Industry cards - Process steps - Feature/value
proposition blocks - CTA bands - Enquiry form - Footer

Keep page content separate from reusable layout components so service
descriptions can be updated without redesigning the entire site.

### Enquiry form fields

Recommended fields: - Full name - Organization / hospital name - Work
email - Phone number - Service required (Staffing / Outsourcing / Data
Centre & IT / Other) - Location - Requirement details - Consent to be
contacted

Include server-side validation, spam protection, clear success/error
feedback and a privacy notice. Do not expose API keys or email
credentials in client-side code.

## 8. SEO and performance

### Suggested homepage metadata

**Title:** Aethelys Healthcare \| Healthcare Staffing, Outsourcing & IT
Solutions

**Description:** Aethelys Healthcare supports hospitals and healthcare
organizations with staffing solutions, outsourced services and
data-centre and IT infrastructure support.

### SEO requirements

-   Unique title and meta description for every page
-   One clear H1 per page
-   Descriptive H2/H3 headings
-   Human-readable URLs
-   Descriptive image alt text
-   Open Graph metadata
-   XML sitemap and robots.txt
-   Canonical URLs
-   Organization schema using verified company details
-   LocalBusiness schema only if appropriate and supported by real
    business information
-   Internal links between service pages and enquiry page

### Performance and accessibility

-   Optimize and properly size images
-   Use responsive image formats
-   Avoid unnecessary client-side JavaScript
-   Lazy-load below-the-fold imagery
-   Target strong Core Web Vitals
-   Semantic HTML and keyboard navigation
-   Accessible form labels and error messages
-   Test on mobile, tablet and desktop

## 9. Content and trust requirements

Before launch, collect and verify: - Official company name and legal
entity details - Approved logo and brand guidelines - Company overview
and leadership biographies - Actual service list and delivery
capabilities - Verified office address, phone and email - Genuine client
testimonials and references, if approved - Real case studies and
measurable outcomes, if documented - Relevant registrations,
certifications and policies - Privacy policy and enquiry-data handling
process

Never invent: - Client names or logos - Staff counts or placement
numbers - Years of experience - Certifications or regulatory approvals -
Partnerships - Performance statistics - Claims of guaranteed clinical
outcomes or uninterrupted infrastructure

Use "proposed", "planned" or "subject to confirmation" internally until
the business owner approves the claim.

**Aethelys Healthcare** using **Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS**.

Use MedSwift Global as inspiration for presenting a broad, clearly
organized healthcare service portfolio. Use AnyMed Services as
inspiration for explaining hospital operations, workforce coordination,
process discipline and accountable service delivery. Do not copy their

pillars:

1.  Healthcare Staffing Solutions
2.  Outsourced Healthcare Services
3.  Data Centre & Healthcare IT Infrastructure Solutions
